The page and the PDF are the same `data/resume.yml`, so they cannot drift the way a hand-maintained pair always does — and the PDF is this page printed, not a second document to keep in step. `_print.scss` is what makes that presentable: it drops the sidebar, the top bar and the footer, un-inverts dark mode, and prints link targets after the text that carried them, since a link is worth nothing on paper. Structure follows jglovier/resume-template: a centred avatar and name, a rule under the headline with the links on the same line, an executive summary, and then sections of title / details / copy. What is *not* taken from it is the typography — that theme loads Open Sans and Lora from a webfont host, and this one committed to the system stack, so the proportions are ported and the font requests are not. Every section is optional. A résumé with no publications should not print a heading with nothing under it, so each one asks first. The microdata is schema.org Person.
